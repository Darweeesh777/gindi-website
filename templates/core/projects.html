Two layers of text: the closed gate's headline, inside the opening, and the open gate's introduction to the work, framed by the parted halves. A downward gesture opens the gate in place; a new one scrolls on to the projects; an upward gesture at the top closes it again. Only the active layer is visible, focusable and announced.
